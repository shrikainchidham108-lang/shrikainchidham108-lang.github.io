Best Chess Openings for Beginners

Published: October 2026 | By A Chess Optimization Expert

Starting a game of chess can feel completely overwhelming if you don't know where to put your pieces. Memorizing deep grandmaster lines isn't necessary when you are starting out. Instead, learning a few of the best chess openings for beginners will help you control the center, develop your pieces quickly, and keep your king completely safe.

1. The Italian Game

The Italian Game is one of the oldest and most reliable chess openings for beginners. It starts with the moves 1.e4 e5, 2.Nf3 Nc6, and 3.Bc4. This opening aims to control the center immediately and positions your light-squared bishop to attack Black's weakest square on f7.

2. The Ruy Lopez (Spanish Opening)

Named after a Spanish priest, the Ruy Lopez is favored by players of all levels. It starts similarly to the Italian Game but moves the white bishop to b5 on the third move. This applies instant pressure to the knight defending Black's center pawn, creating a long-term strategic advantage.

3. The Four Knights Game

If you prefer a highly stable, symmetrical layout where pieces are brought out safely, the Four Knights Game is excellent. It focuses completely on fundamental opening principles: controlling the center board and developing all your knights early without overextending.

💡 Top Beginner Chess Tip:

Never move the same piece twice during the opening phase unless you are absolutely forced to. Focus entirely on controlling the 4 center squares (d4, d5, e4, e5) and castle your king as early as possible!
