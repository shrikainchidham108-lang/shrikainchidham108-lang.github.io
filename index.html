Best Budget Running Shoes for Flat Feet

Published on October 27, 2026 | Written by An Expert

Finding the right footwear can be a painful challenge if you have flat feet. Most high-stability shoes cost a fortune, but you don't have to overspend to get great arch support. In this guide, we review the absolute best budget running shoes for flat feet that offer comfort without destroying your wallet.

Why Flat Feet Need Specific Running Shoes

When you have flat feet, your arches collapse inward while running. This is called overpronation. To fix this, search for shoes that provide structured arch support and medial post stability.
